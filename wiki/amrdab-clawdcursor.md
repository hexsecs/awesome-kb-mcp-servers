Desktop control for any AI agent on Windows, macOS and Linux. Reads the screen as text (accessibility tree, then OCR) and acts on controls by name, with screenshots only as a last resort, so a task costs a fraction of the tokens of a screenshot loop. 7 compound tools, one safety gate, verifies its own actions, and pauses for the user at sign-ins. Local-only, any tool-calling model.
