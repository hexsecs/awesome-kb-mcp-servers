Turns walking distance into a meal calorie budget (450 kcal + 4 kcal per 80 m; 800 m ≈ +44 kcal, about half an apple). Lists nearby restaurants and their recommended dishes, marks each dish as fits-or-over the budget, and abstains instead of guessing. Map and recipe data are Chinese; McDonald's nutrition comes from the official China MCP.
