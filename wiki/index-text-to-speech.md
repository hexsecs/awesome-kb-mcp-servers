# Text-to-Speech

- [daisys-ai/daisys-mcp](daisys-ai-daisys-mcp.md) — Generate text-to-speech and text-to-voice audio with the DAISYS platform, and play and store the generated audio.
- [fasuizu-br/brainiall-mcp-server](fasuizu-br-brainiall-mcp-server.md) — Speech tools: pronunciation assessment with phoneme-level feedback, speech-to-text with language detection and text-to-speech with multiple voices.
- [forgemeshlabs/voice-mcp](forgemeshlabs-voice-mcp.md) — Pay-per-call x402 speech generation on Base: multiple voices, personas and languages, speed and quality controls, OpenAI-style requests and batch audio.
- [loudreader/loudkit](loudreader-loudkit.md) — Local text-to-speech from an on-device model with multiple voices and languages, cloned voice profiles and WAV, FLAC, MP3 or Opus output.
- [mbailey/voice-mcp](mbailey-voice-mcp.md) — Voice interaction: speech-to-text, text-to-speech and real-time voice conversations via local microphone, OpenAI-compatible APIs and LiveKit.
- [mberg/kokoro-tts-mcp](mberg-kokoro-tts-mcp.md) — Text-to-speech with the open-weight Kokoro TTS models, saving MP3s locally or uploading them to an S3 bucket.
- [transcribe-app/mcp-transcribe](transcribe-app-mcp-transcribe.md) — Transcribe audio and video files and voice memos and work with their text content.
- [ybouhjira/claude-code-tts](ybouhjira-claude-code-tts.md) — Text-to-speech for Claude Code using OpenAI's TTS API, with multiple voices and cross-platform playback.
