Synthesizes 26 ancient divination systems (BaZi, Vedic Jyotish, Western, Nine Star Ki, Thai Seven Number, Mayan, Norse Runes, Human Design and 18 more) into a single consensus Cosmic Score showing where the traditions agree vs contradict. Deterministic (no LLM in the math), fully local — no birth data leaves the process. Official MCP for mythsensus.com. Install: `npx -y mythsensus-mcp`.
