- [vectara/vectara-mcp](vectara-vectara-mcp.md) — Access Vectara's RAG-as-a-service platform.
- [zaharajabeen13-create/ai-rete-rag-mcp](zaharajabeen13-create-ai-rete-rag-mcp.md) — Rule-based decisions: a Rete engine returns verdicts explained via retrieval over your policy documents, with rules drafted from policy text and saved as YAML.
