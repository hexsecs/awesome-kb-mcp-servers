Let an agent ask its owner first, on their phone, before doing something irreversible. `ask_approval` blocks until a person answers and distinguishes DENIED from NO ANSWER, so a timeout is never read as consent; a fingerprint can be required, and the answer records that a human gave it. `send_notification` covers one-way alerts with priorities and quiet hours. Works with Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf or anything else that speaks MCP — `npx -y lauther-mcp`.
