# Podcasts

- [conorbronsdon/apple-podcasts-mcp](conorbronsdon-apple-podcasts-mcp.md) — Read-only Apple Podcasts analytics for shows you own via Podcasts Connect Reporter: plays, unique and engaged listeners, followers and per-episode listening.
- [conorbronsdon/op3-mcp](conorbronsdon-op3-mcp.md) — Read-only podcast analytics from OP3 (Open Podcast Prefix Project): downloads over time, listener geography, app share and per-episode breakdowns.
- [conorbronsdon/podcastindex-mcp](conorbronsdon-podcastindex-mcp.md) — Search the Podcast Index: podcasts, episodes, guest appearances by person, trending shows and feed health.
- [ni-c/audiobookshelf-mcp](ni-c-audiobookshelf-mcp.md) — Browse an Audiobookshelf library, query listening history and stats, and sync progress, bookmarks, collections and playlists, with optional read-only mode.
