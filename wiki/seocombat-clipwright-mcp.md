Make UGC-style video ads without filming: tell your AI assistant what to say and get a vertical clip of a realistic actor saying it, ready for TikTok, Reels or Shorts. Pick or describe an actor, choose a voice from samples, and see the price before rendering. It also makes faceless videos from a script or a short brief. `npx -y @clipwright/mcp-server`
