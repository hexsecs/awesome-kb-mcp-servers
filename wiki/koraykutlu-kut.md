Local-first personal CRM that keeps your contacts as plain Markdown files. Read-only MCP server (`kut.pyw --mcp`): search people, open a contact card, see who to reach out to this week, recent conversations and a meeting brief. Contacts marked private are never listed, secrets are masked, and phone numbers and emails are never returned.
