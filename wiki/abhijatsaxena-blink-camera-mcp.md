Control Amazon Blink cameras and their pan/tilt mount: read where the camera is pointing and its travel limits, aim it by absolute or relative angle with the new position confirmed by the hardware, return it home, and grab a snapshot. The mount's command channel is undocumented, so it was recovered from the Blink app's bytecode and verified against a real camera. `uvx blink-camera-mcp`
