# Home Automation

- [handsomejustin/mijia-control](handsomejustin-mijia-control.md) — Control Xiaomi/Mijia smart home devices (lights, AC, heaters, robots, cameras), with a web dashboard, REST API, CLI, energy monitoring and automation rules.
- [apiarya/wemo-mcp-server](apiarya-wemo-mcp-server.md) — Local control of WeMo smart home devices without the cloud: discovery, dimmer brightness, device renaming and HomeKit codes.
- [Hybirdss/smartest-tv](hybirdss-smartest-tv.md) — Control LG, Samsung, Android TV and Roku smart TVs: play Netflix, YouTube and Spotify content by name, cast URLs, scene presets and multi-TV sync.
- [kambriso/fritzbox-mcp-server](kambriso-fritzbox-mcp-server.md) — Control AVM FRITZ!Box routers: manage devices, Wi-Fi, network settings and parental controls, and schedule time-delayed actions.
- [ober37/ac-infinity-mcp](ober37-ac-infinity-mcp.md) — Monitor and automate AC Infinity grow controllers: live sensor data and history, VPD/temperature/humidity automations, port control and scheduling.
- [laszlopere/mcp-kodi](laszlopere-mcp-kodi.md) — Control Kodi media players via JSON-RPC: playback, volume, library search, queue management and playback history across multiple instances.
- [claymore666/debmatic-mcp](claymore666-debmatic-mcp.md) — Control a HomeMatic/debmatic CCU: switch and dim actuators, read sensors and system variables, run programs and manage rooms, links and devices.
- [NickoScope/nickol-knx-mcp](nickoscope-nickol-knx-mcp.md) — Design-time KNX/ETS6 assistant: read-only .knxproj parsing, DPT, naming and command/status validation, and Home Assistant YAML and ETS exports.
- [alexpfau/zigbee2mqtt-mcp](alexpfau-zigbee2mqtt-mcp.md) — Administer Zigbee2MQTT via its MQTT bridge API: network health reports, mesh topology, pairing, binding, reporting intervals and device options.
