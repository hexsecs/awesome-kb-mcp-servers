Deterministic attack-surface perception for AI security agents. Attaches over `chrome.debugger` to the Chrome you're already logged into (cookies, MFA and SSO already done) and turns the live page into a short numbered interaction graph an agent can act on (`[03] button 'Sign In'`), with CSS selectors kept server-side and out of the model's context. Cross-identity tools (`compare_identities`, `replay`) flag a byte-identical response across two logged-in users as a cross-tenant IDOR, and `scan` maps the attack surface from the CLI. No LLM in the parse; 71% fewer tokens than Playwright's ARIA snapshot mode.
